About SAMRIDH-AI & Project Architecture

Smart Agriculture Monitoring, Risk Insurance Decision Hub & Agronomy AI System.

Designed & Developed by Team TwinBit

National PMFBY Agri-Decision Support System

SAMRIDH-AI is an advanced AI and IoT-powered decision support system designed specifically for the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY). By addressing the critical bottleneck in crop loss estimation — the reliance on subjective visual inspection and disputed Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) — SAMRIDH-AI establishes a continuous, tamper-proof Digital Farm Health Ledger.

🛠️ Technical Tools & Frameworks Used

📡

1. IoT Edge Root-Zone Sensors

TwinBit SoilNode Pro: Custom firmware on ESP32 microcontrollers with capacitive SDI-12 root permittivity probes, RS485 telemetry, and solar-buffered LiFePO4 battery banks.

🛰️

2. Satellite Multispectral Remote Sensing

ESA Sentinel-2 & RISAT SAR: Automated pipeline calculating 10m Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), NDRE, and cloud-penetrating synthetic aperture radar for flood inundation mapping.

👁️

3. Computer Vision & Quality Gating

OpenCV & SegFormer: Laplacian variance blur quality gates, Excess Green (ExG) chromatic canopy extraction, and deep semantic segmentation for crop lodging damage estimation.

🔒

4. Anti-Fraud & Perceptual Hashing

64-bit dHash & pHash: Hamming distance checks preventing recycled web images, combined with strict PostGIS geofence polygon boundary inclusion checks.

⚡

5. FastAPI & Spatial Database

FastAPI (Python 3.11) & PostgreSQL: Asynchronous REST architecture with SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM, GeoAlchemy2, JWT stateless authentication, and Docker containerization.

📱

6. PWA & Cross-Platform Client

Tailwind CSS & Service Workers: Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline caching, Leaflet.js interactive GIS maps, Chart.js telemetry graphs, and Android APK deployment scripts.

📖 How It Works: Role-by-Role Guidelines

👨‍🌾 1. For Farmers (किसान गाइड):
  • Check Real-Time Health: Monitor soil moisture and canopy vigor received from your field IoT SoilNode.
  • Report Calamity Damage (72h SOS): In case of flood, hailstorm, or lodging, tap Report Damage, snap ground photos, and submit within 72 hours.
  • AI Crop Doctor: Upload leaf photos to detect fungal/pest infections and get instant treatment advisories in Hindi or English.
📋 2. For District Agriculture Officers & Insurer Loss Assessors:
  • Triage Queue: Review incoming claims with auto-computed GPS Geofence compliance, pHash fraud scores, and SIFT baseline matching.
  • Before/After Comparison: Compare pre-calamity sowing baseline photos with post-disaster AI segmentation masks.
  • Sanction & DBT: Approve claims to generate automated PFMS DBT disbursement dockets.
🗺️ 3. For State Nodal Officers & MNCFC Administrators:
  • National Geospatial Grid: View state-wide parcel health, flood inundation layers, and aggregated loss exposure in real-time.
  • Disaster Footprint Audits: Correlate claim density with meteorological radar alerts to prevent systemic fraud.
